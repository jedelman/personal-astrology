# Personal Astrology Site for Jason Edelman

Jason Edelman is an astrologer that uses humanistic astrology as a method of inquiry to assist people in navigating the challenges of their lives. This site helps interested clients determine whether his approach is right for them. The main call to action will be to book an initial consult, but the goal is to secure long-term clients. 

## Jason's Approach

Jason's approach to astrology is as a method of revealing the larger patterns in one's life - but rather than pointing them out directly to the client, Jason acts as a guide through their own life, using the chart as a compass to map the contours of their inner and outer journey. In this way, his astrology is a form of pedagogy, aimed at developing broad perspective, empathy, and most of all *agency* in the way that it uniquely expresses through each individual client.
